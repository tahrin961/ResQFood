# 🍽️ ResQFood

> **Rescuing Surplus. Feeding Value. Reducing Waste.**

ResQFood is a digital marketplace that connects restaurants, bakeries, grocery stores, and event caterers with nearby consumers to sell surplus food at discounted prices before it goes to waste.

Instead of throwing away perfectly edible food, vendors recover part of their revenue, customers save money, and any remaining surplus can be redirected to verified NGO partners.

---

## 🌍 The Problem

Bangladesh generates millions of tonnes of food waste every year while many households continue to face food insecurity.

Restaurants, bakeries, supermarkets, and event organizers often discard perfectly edible food simply because there is no efficient platform to redistribute it before it expires.

Current food delivery platforms focus on freshly prepared meals—not surplus food.

ResQFood bridges this gap.

---

## 💡 Solution

ResQFood provides a real-time marketplace where vendors can quickly list surplus food and nearby users can purchase it at discounted prices.

The platform creates value for everyone involved:

- 🏪 Vendors recover revenue from unsold food.
- 👨‍👩‍👧 Consumers access quality meals at affordable prices.
- 🌱 Food waste is significantly reduced.
- 🤝 Remaining surplus can be donated to NGO partners.
